Piano Multiphonic Calculator

Developed version of Caspar Johannes Walter’s Piano Multiphonic Calculator, integrating Juhani T. Vesikkala’s research on grand piano multiphonics (2016). Published with kind permission of Caspar Johannes Walter.

Sources

Walter, C. J. (n.d.). Piano multiphonic calculator [Web application]. https://www.casparjohanneswalter.de/research/piano_multiphonic_calculator

Vesikkala, J. T. (2016). Multiphonics of the grand piano – Timbral composition and performance with flageolets. Sibelius Academy.

all nodes of one string (Vesikkala 2016, Appendix 3)

Tap a row to take the node (string, octave, the two partials) into the active string. Strike plays the node with piano synthesis (with “Audible only”: only the partials of the group that sound at the node; the notation shows them). Partials: the upper three or four of the group, hover for all. Vesikkala stops at 19 by assumption.
